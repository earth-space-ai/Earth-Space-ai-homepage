Methodology and status definitions

Evidence policy

Only provider, agency, facility, foundation, or provider-linked application pages were accepted as evidence. Aggregators were not used.

Endpoint rule

Endpoint liveness and program openness are checked separately. A live page or form can belong to a closed or repurposed call; a moved page can point to a valid new route.

Status definitions

Open
An official page exposed a current intake or stated that requests could be submitted on the snapshot date.
Upcoming
An official page promised a future round, while an intake or application route was not yet available.
Closed
The named deadline passed or the official page said that intake was closed, even if the page remained available.
