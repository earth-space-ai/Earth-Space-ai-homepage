Methodology and status definitions

Evidence policy

Only provider, agency, facility, foundation, or provider-linked application pages were accepted as evidence. Aggregators were not used. The all-opportunities view contains verified records from the documented searched-source universe, not unverified search noise or an internet-wide exhaustive list.

Endpoint rule

Endpoint liveness and program openness are checked separately. A live page or form can belong to an archived or repurposed call; a moved page can point to a valid new route. Application controls are suppressed for tracker-archived records even when an official endpoint remains live.

Status definitions

Open
Official evidence showed current intake and the modeled cycle remained outside the tracker’s 15-day lead-time archive fence.
Upcoming
Official evidence promised a future round that remained outside the archive fence, while intake or an application route was not yet available.
Archived
The sponsor closed intake, the named cycle passed, or the tracker’s inclusive 15-day lead-time policy moved the cycle out of the actionable view. Policy-retired cards carry a visible lifecycle reason; legacy archive cards may instead explain status in their deadline or verification notes.

Independent, best-effort resource. This tracker is not affiliated with or endorsed by listed providers. Always confirm deadlines, eligibility, and terms before acting. The exact public dataset is available as public_opportunities.json, with a deterministic provenance manifest.
